How quagen.lol and Quagen Studios / Project Q collect, use, and protect information.

2. What This Website Does

  • Displays project and release information (including changelogs)

  • Provides contact and support options

You may choose to provide information when you:

  • Submit a support or help request

This information may include a username, email address, or message content. We use it only to respond to your request.

4. Information Collected Automatically

When you visit quagen.lol, limited technical information may be collected automatically for basic operation, security, and analytics purposes:

  • IP address (typically logged by hosting/security providers)

  • Referrer URL and visit timestamps
  • Performance and error data

We do not intentionally collect sensitive personal data such as government IDs, payment information, or precise location data.
